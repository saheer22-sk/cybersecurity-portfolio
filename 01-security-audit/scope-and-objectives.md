# NovaMart Cybersecurity Security Audit

## Audit Purpose

The purpose of this security audit is to identify cybersecurity
risks and weaknesses within NovaMart's systems, protect customer
and company information, and recommend security controls that can
improve the overall security of the organization.

## Audit Scope

The security audit covers:

- 25 Windows computers
- Company Wi-Fi network
- E-commerce website
- Customer/order database
- Cloud file storage
- Employee accounts
- Administrator account
- Router and firewall
- CCTV system
- Customer and company data

## Information Requiring Protection

NovaMart needs to protect:

- Customer names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Order information
- Employee information
- Authentication information
- Business documents
- System and configuration information
- Database information

## Potential Security Impact

Poor cybersecurity could result in unauthorized access, data
breaches, loss or modification of company information, system
downtime, financial losses, and loss of customer trust.

In a serious security incident, attackers could gain control of
important company systems and disrupt business operations.

## CIA Triad Considerations

**Confidentiality:** Customer, employee, and company information
must only be accessible to authorized users.

**Integrity:** Customer orders, company records, and system data
must be protected against unauthorized modification.

**Availability:** The website, database, network, and other
important systems should remain available to authorized users
when required.
