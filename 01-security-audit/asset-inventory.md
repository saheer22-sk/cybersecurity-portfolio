# NovaMart Asset Inventory and Classification

This asset inventory identifies important NovaMart assets and
classifies them according to their importance to business operations
and cybersecurity.

| Asset | Type | Importance | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer Database | Data | Critical | Contains customer and order information that requires confidentiality and integrity protection. |
| E-commerce Website | Application | High | Supports customer access and online transactions. A security incident could affect customers and business operations. |
| Windows Computers | Hardware | High | Employees depend on these computers for daily business activities and access to company systems. |
| Company Wi-Fi / Network | Network | High | A compromised network could allow unauthorized access, data exposure, or disruption of company operations. |
| Administrator Account | Privileged Account | Critical | Provides high-level access to systems and configurations. Compromise could give an attacker significant control. |
| Employee Accounts | Accounts | High | Compromised employee accounts could provide unauthorized access to company systems and information. |
| Cloud File Storage | Cloud / Data | Critical | Stores important business files and information that require confidentiality, integrity, and availability. |
| Router / Firewall | Network / Security | Critical | Protects and connects the company network. Compromise could expose internal systems or disrupt network access. |
| CCTV System | Physical Security | Medium | Supports monitoring and physical security of the company environment. |
| Customer Information | Data | Critical | Contains sensitive customer information that must be protected against unauthorized access or modification. |
